# 10-minute user test

Goal: get one or two people who run launches, releases or programs to try Launch Control before the interview, and write down what they say. Real reactions beat any feature.

**Who to ask:** a product ops, program, release or launch manager; a PM who has run a go/no-go; or a QA or support lead who has been on the receiving end of one.

**Setup (1 min):** send https://austinjw.vercel.app. Say: "I built a tool for launch readiness. I'm not testing you; I want to see where it's confusing. Think out loud."

## Tasks (6 min). Don't help; note where they hesitate.
1. "Without clicking anything: which launch would you worry about, and why?"
2. "Find out what exactly is blocking that launch and who owns it."
3. "You need to tell your VP where this launch stands. Use the page to do that." (They need the passcode for the agent; give it if they reach for it.)
4. "Something is wrong with the tracker data itself. Find it and fix what you can."

## Questions (3 min)
- "What would you use this for in your real job, if anything?"
- "What's missing before you'd trust it for a real launch?"
- "What on the page did you not understand or not believe?"
- "How do you do this today, and how long does it take?"
